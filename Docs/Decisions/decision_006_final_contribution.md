# Decision 006 — Final Contribution Boundary

**Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Status:** Approved for Milestone 0 completion  
**Supersedes:** Provisional contribution language in earlier charter versions

## Frozen contribution

> Disclosure Drift is a preregistered temporal-reliability study testing whether models developed
> exclusively on pre-2022 Form 10-K disclosures lose predictive accuracy or calibration when
> forecasting one-year-ahead, industry-adjusted operating-margin deterioration in the untouched 2024
> filing cohort; whether any reliability loss is concentrated in style-dependent representations and
> associated with a frozen Disclosure Drift Index; and whether evidence-grounded models are more
> stable than style-heavy models under both observed disclosure change and paired,
> fact-preserving rewrites. The design includes a prospective 2025 replication and outcome-free 2026
> monitoring with timestamped frozen predictions.

## Why this contribution remains defensible

No study in the updated 62-source literature universe combines all of the following:

1. models developed only on pre-2022 10-K filings;
2. a mature untouched 2024 test;
3. one-year industry-adjusted operating-margin change;
4. accuracy and calibration decay;
5. financial-only, style-heavy, and evidence-grounded model families;
6. an outcome-free AI-era language proxy;
7. paired factual-preservation rewrites applied to frozen models;
8. a prospective 2025 replication; and
9. 2026 outcome-free monitoring with frozen predictions.

## Claims permanently prohibited

The project must not claim to be the first:

- temporal-drift study using 10-K prediction;
- out-of-sample audit of predictive information in 10-K text;
- future-outcome, bankruptcy, operating-risk, tax, or financial-performance prediction study using
  filing narrative;
- financial semantic-drift framework;
- study of AI-assisted or AI-like financial reporting;
- controlled transformation of financial narrative;
- full-10-K versus Item 1A predictive comparison; or
- evidence-grounded financial NLP system.

The project also must not claim verified AI authorship, fraud, deception, or causal GenAI effects from
post-2022 timing alone.

## Publication safeguard

Run a current literature refresh:

1. before the first public model-results release;
2. before submitting the research paper;
3. before recording the final demo; and
4. whenever a new direct competitor is identified.

The Lin (2026) full paper must be rechecked when accessible.

Milestone 0 is complete for progression to Milestone 1. Research implementation remains governed by
