## Objective

Purpose: implement the **pure, in-memory accession candidate and joint entity-accession selection
core** — the accession-level analogue of the existing S4.1 `entity_selector.py` — so that entity and
accession quotas are solved as one joint assignment problem rather than sequentially. Governed
primarily by Decision 013 §5 (selector policy, D10) and
[Decision 018](../../Docs/Decisions/decision_018_m23_s5_accession_selection_policy.md) (accession
selection policy, approved 2026-07-28).

**Exact existing accepted objective terms (Decision 013 §5, quoted verbatim — this contract adds
nothing to them):**

> a lexicographic objective (plan §8.4): zero unmet hard quotas, then minimize unresolved/provisional
> evidence, then minimize base-accession count, then stress-accession count, then the ordered vector
> of entity hashes, then the ordered vector of accession hashes;

Decision 013 also already requires: integer/categorical comparisons only (no floating-point
objective), explicit fixed input ordering, deterministic branch ordering, a deterministic
search-node limit, and `infeasible_or_unproven` on exhaustion — never a partial manifest. These
terms are accepted and are not reopened by this contract or by Decision 018.

**Decision 018 has frozen the accession-specific interpretations** (approved 2026-07-28). It
clarifies the terms above without reordering or splitting them, and it states explicitly that it does
not retrofit or alter the accepted S4 entity selector or any S4 persisted artifact (§3.3). The
previously open items are enumerated with their governing sections under "Frozen by Decision 018"
below. S5.1 is now implementable from Decision 013 §5 together with Decision 018 — but only once a
separate implementation prompt authorizes it.

## In scope

**S5.1 is the pure core only.** Exactly two things:

- A pure, in-memory accession-candidate representation and joint entity-accession solver, structured
  like `entity_selector.py` (S4.1): **no SQLite access of any kind**, no network calls, no filing
  text, no outcomes, no clock read. Per
  [Decision 018 §19](../../Docs/Decisions/decision_018_m23_s5_accession_selection_policy.md), the
  pure core owns the deterministic policy functions for: accession role assignment; applicability-
  aware evidence penalties; amendment-family derivation; fiscal-year-end-change derivation;
  name-change contribution; quota contribution and diagnostics; and joint entity-accession
  optimization. Decision 018 §19 also requires that **the SQLite adapter must not become a second
  methodological implementation** — each of these rules lives here and nowhere else.
- Adversarial unit tests for that solver, in-memory only.

Nothing else. In particular, S5.1 does **not** include SQLite access, accession-selection
persistence, `accession_selection_store.py`, frozen-snapshot reconstruction, run-lifecycle writes,
quota-result persistence, or selected-accession persistence — every one of those is S5.2 below.

## Required adversarial test categories (on a separate implementation prompt)

All are in-memory unit tests over the pure solver. No test under this contract opens SQLite, and
none asserts reserve behavior (S5.4). Decision 018 §30 states the full required-test list; the
categories below are this contract's subset of it, and it is the controlling record where the two
differ.

- Fail-closed `infeasible_or_unproven` on a genuinely infeasible joint pool, with an empty selection
  (never a partial result).
- Deterministic reproducibility: the same candidate input set produces the same selected entity and
  accession lists across repeated runs and across input orderings.
- Joint-objective interaction: a case where an entity-optimal choice is not accession-optimal, to
  prove the objective is solved jointly rather than sequentially.
- Multi-registrant accession quota satisfied exactly once regardless of registrant-CIK count
  (Decision 013 §4, D9).
- Search-node-limit exhaustion path, including exhaustion after a feasible incumbent was found
  internally — every incumbent is discarded and no approved selection is returned
  (Decision 018 §17).
- Malformed and duplicate accession inputs rejected before any search begins.
- Per-CIK and global accession cap enforcement (Decision 018 §8; the same limits previously cited as
  plan §4.2).
- Canonical hashing determinism across process runs, against the frozen Decision 018 §5.2 formula.
- Role assignment is total and mutually exclusive; 10-KT and 10-KT/A classify as stress, never base;
  an unclassifiable accession is rejected (Decision 018 §7).
- Applicability-aware evidence penalty: `provisional` scores 0, weaker scores 1, structurally
  inapplicable scores 0, and a valid 2009 support accession receives **no** cohort penalty
  (Decision 018 §3.4). Term 2 is a single integer with no floating-point value anywhere in its
  computation (§3.2).
- Entity accession floors: every operating entity has a base accession, every control entity has a
  control-role accession, every selected entity anchors at least one accession (Decision 018 §9).
- Family derivation over transitive chains, including cycle rejection and singleton isolation;
  linked-amendment coverage requires the resolved root original to be co-selected
  (Decision 018 §10).
- Fiscal-year-end derivation uses **circular** month-day distance and handles the year boundary;
  drift within seven days does not contribute; missing `report_date` fails closed
  (Decision 018 §12).
- Name-change contribution requires provisional winning identity evidence; a ticker-only claim does
  not contribute and its absence alone produces no warning (Decision 018 §13).
- 2009/2010 pairing requires one anchor CIK, one support and one target accession, counted once per
  distinct entity (Decision 018 §15).
- The unmeasurable difficult-or-nonstandard-package quota is excluded from feasibility and reported
  as `unproven`/`unavailable` with `achieved_count = 0` and `eligible_pool_count = 0`, and a feasible
  run remains reachable with it outstanding (Decision 018 §14).

`comparison_operator`-aware quota evaluation and composite `(selection_run_id, snapshot_id)`
foreign-key integrity (Decision 016 §6) are **persistence** properties and belong to S5.2's test set,
not this stage's. So are the loader fail-closed obligations (Decision 018 §5.3) and the gate failure
on a same-ID `failed`/`planned`/incomplete-`running` row (Decision 018 §18).
