## 9. Request-plan and ceiling controls

- The run **consumes** the approved plan; it never re-derives or recomputes one mid-window
  (master plan M3.2 §15).
- `m3 acquire` requires `--plan` (matching hash `19be7bdc…`), `--window M3.2A`, an explicit
  `--live` flag with no default, and `--ceiling 801` exactly equal to the approved integer; a
  mismatch on any of these refuses before any transport construction.
- The shared cumulative physical-attempt ceiling gate (`sec.request_ceiling`) is supplied
  explicitly — never defaulted or inferred — and **refuses the attempt that would exceed 801**:
  stop-before-overflow, never stop-after. A complete run may finish exactly at 801; equality with
  planned work remaining is `stopped_at_ceiling`, records `SEC_REQUEST_CEILING_EXHAUSTED`, and is
  a Gate H failure. The ceiling is **never raised mid-window**; more headroom requires stopping,
  re-planning, and a new owner approval.
- Every retry, redirect hop, and controlled post-cooldown request consumes one physical attempt;
  the accepted response policy applies unchanged (5 transient retries, 60 s backoff ceiling,
  `Retry-After` honoured when usable, one 600 s aggregate cooldown with one controlled further
  request, second cooldown terminal; retryable statuses 408/500/502/503/504; failure never becomes
  an empty success).
- Actual counts are recorded per route and reconciled item-by-item against the plan at Gate H; a
  deviation is a finding, not a variance.

## 12. Interruption, restart, and recovery

Per master plan M3.2 §§27–29 and `interrupted_run_recovery.md`: on interruption, stop new
requests; preserve every attempt and committed object; quarantine partial/unverifiable objects;
roll back the uncommitted transaction; rebuild the projection; run integrity and FK checks; write
the terminating receipt; **require an explicit owner resume-or-new-run decision**. Resume only
after the M3.1 read-only inspector (`m3 recovery-state`) reports `SAFE` and duplicate prevention
is proven; the consumed physical-attempt count carries forward under the same ceiling; a resumed
receipt names its predecessor. **Conservative accounting for unrecorded attempts (Decision 032):**
a hard interruption that wrote no terminating receipt leaves the in-flight logical request's
physical attempts unrecorded — resume accounting must charge that request at its full per-route
`A_reachable` against the ceiling, and where even that bound cannot be established the
determination is `UNDETERMINED`. **Recovery uncertainty is a stop condition** — if it cannot be
determined whether a write committed, the run does not resume. Nothing acquired is ever deleted.

## 14. M3.2A termination, successful completion, and Gate H

**Termination.** M3.2A **terminates** when every planned logical request has reached a terminal
disposition — satisfied, reused, or terminally classified with a registered reason — and the
window's terminating receipt validates. **Termination alone is never success** (Decision 032,
finding F1).

**Successful completion.** M3.2A **completes successfully** only when, additionally: **every
required object is present in the raw store, hash-verified, and fully provenanced** — the
bulk-submissions object, both ticker files, the SIC list, the calendar-year filing-calendar page,
every approved announcement-manifest entry (zero in the approved plan), and all 70 required
quarterly-index instances; **any planned request whose terminal disposition left its required
object absent** (an absent-evidence `404`, a quarantined body, or any terminal failure) **is
enumerated in the window's receipt and expressly adjudicated by the owner before the
between-windows freeze and before any M3.2B budget approval** — a window with any unadjudicated
required-object absence is `completed_with_absences`, is **not** successfully complete, and is
**not** eligible for the freeze/derivation step or for Gate H; actual counts reconcile to the plan
(`actual ≤ ceiling`, zero overflow); zero prohibited-route attempts; zero unresolved blocking
drift; network disabled again; every receipt validated; and the between-windows freeze recorded
the exact bootstrap object identities.

**Gate H** (canonical name; `gate_h_checklist.md`) runs only after M3.2B and integrates **both**
windows: per-window planned-versus-actual reconciliation, raw-store and provenance completeness,
classification totals, drift outcomes, recovery records, receipts, and the network-disabled
confirmations. Gate H checklist item 3.3 ("completed its whole approved plan") is read under this
section's successful-completion standard — a window completes its plan only by acquiring every
required object or carrying an express owner adjudication for each absence; the frozen template
itself is not edited. Phase token, emitted only by the canonical command path once every Gate H
item passes: `M3_2_METADATA_ACQUISITION_COMPLETE_GATE_H_PASSED`.
